## Payment Structure

### Discord Server Setup

- 50% paid upfront before work begins

- Remaining 50% due once the setup is completed

### Discord Bot Development

- 50% paid upfront before development begins

- A semi completed version may be provided during development for review

- Full source code is delivered only after the remaining 50% is paid

### Discord Server Review & Maintenance

- 100% paid upfront before the review begins

Looking for available payment methods? Check the dedicated **Payment
Methods** page for details.

---

## Refund Policy

Once work has started on your project, **refunds are not possible**.

If you wish to cancel before work begins, your upfront payment is
refundable. Note that exchange or transaction fees may apply depending on
the payment method and currency used, and these fees will be deducted from
the refunded amount.

---

## Revisions

Each service plan includes a set number of revisions, outlined at the time
of order. Once that revision limit is used, any further changes will be
treated as additional work and billed separately.
