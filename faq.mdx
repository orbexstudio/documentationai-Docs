## Do you offer ongoing support after delivery?

Yes, but it depends on the plan. Starter Setup includes 1 day of
post-delivery support, Premium Setup includes 3 days, and Complete
Optimisation includes 3 days. Review Plan and Bot Development do not
include post-delivery support by default. Anything beyond the included
support window is treated as additional work and discussed separately.

---

## Can I see examples of past work?

Yes. We have live preview servers set up for both the Starter and Premium
server setup plans so you can explore them before deciding. Invite links
are available in our main [Discord server](https://discord.gg/srar9fFaNT).

---

## What if I'm not satisfied with the result?

Each plan includes a set number of free revisions within a time window,
so if something doesn't feel right after delivery, that's what revisions
are for. If you're unsatisfied beyond the revision window, reach out via
a ticket and we'll do our best to sort it out. We care about the quality
of our work and won't just leave you hanging.

---

## Can I cancel my order after paying?

If work has not started yet, your upfront payment is refundable. Note
that exchange or transaction fees may be deducted depending on the payment
method used. Once work has started, refunds are no longer possible. See
our [Payment & Refund Policy](/payment-refund-policy) for full details.

---

## Do I need coding knowledge to use a bot built by Orbex?

No. Bots we build using BotForge are designed so that clients can maintain
them without deep coding knowledge. In most cases you only need to set
values like your bot token and a few environment variables. Nothing more.

---

## Do you host the bot for me?

No, bot hosting is not included in our service. Once the bot is delivered,
you are responsible for hosting it yourself. If you're unsure where to
start, feel free to ask in a ticket and we can point you in the right
direction.

---

## How long does it take to get a response after opening a ticket?

We aim to respond to all tickets as soon as possible. Response times may
vary depending on workload, but we'll always acknowledge your ticket and
keep you updated.

---

## Still have a question?

If something isn't covered here, open a ticket in our
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/srar9fFaNT) and we'll get back to you.
