## Available Methods

| Method  | Currency                     | Region     |
| ------- | ---------------------------- | ---------- |
| bKash   | BDT                          | Bangladesh |
| UPI     | INR                          | India      |
| SadaPay | PKR                          | Pakistan   |
| eSewa   | NPR                          | Nepal      |
| Crypto  | BTC, ETH, USDT, LTC and more | Worldwide  |

**Litecoin (LTC)** is our preferred crypto option, but other major coins
like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT are accepted as well.

Actual payment details (account numbers, wallet addresses, etc.) are not
listed here and will be shared privately once you're ready to place an
order.

---

## Exchange Fees

Payments made in **BDT via bKash** have no additional exchange fee.

For all other currencies and crypto, a small exchange fee applies. Since
our agent manually converts the payment between currencies (similar to how
services like Wise work), this fee covers that conversion process.

---

## How Payment Works

1. Open a ticket in our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/srar9fFaNT)

2. We'll share the relevant payment details based on your preferred method

3. Complete the payment

4. Provide a **screenshot** and the **transaction ID** as proof of payment

Once verified, your order will move forward according to our
[Payment & Refund Policy](/payment-refund-policy).

Got a payment method that's not listed? Reach out through a ticket and
we'll see what we can work out.
